Bond-length evolution over the extracted trajectory

0–360° retains the spatial angle magnitude and uses the reference normal to choose direction; an in-plane normal is rejected. Folded mode maps the usual 0–180° bond angle modulo 180°.

—
Bond-angle evolution over the extracted trajectory

Torsion about the middle bond, in ASE's 0–360° convention. Periodic-image correction is optional in Crystal cell. Collinear groups have undefined dihedrals.

Folded mode maps torsions modulo 180° into [−90°, +90°); +90° is represented by −90°. Opposite orientations become equivalent. Change the range, then recompute.

—
Dihedral-angle evolution over the active trajectory

Low off-diagonal RMSD means the trajectory revisits earlier states; blocks along the diagonal show distinct basins. Use a frame step close to the decorrelation stride to compare non-correlated configurations.

—
Pairwise RMSD between analysed frames

Normalised g(r) with minimum-image distances; needs a periodic cell. Rmax is limited to half the smallest cell width. n(r) is the running coordination number.

—
Radial distribution function

Positions are unwrapped when a periodic cell is present; MSD is averaged over all time origins; D = slope / 6 from a linear fit in the chosen window.

—
Mean-square displacement vs lag time

Fourier transform of finite-difference velocities (Hann window), normalised to unit area. Save frames every MD step (or few steps) for accurate high frequencies; the Nyquist limit is shown after the calculation.

—
Vibrational density of states

Dihedrals are unwrapped across 0/360° before the linear correlation. τ gives the decorrelation time: sampling configurations every ≥ τ yields statistically independent structures.
